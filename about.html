Sarasota carpentry you can count on

Welcome to Coastal Carpentry & Cabinet, where fine craftsmanship meets local dedication. Sarasota has been our home since 2004. Our founder John Helwig II began crafting woodwork across the Suncoast in 2014, eventually establishing Coastal Carpentry & Cabinet in 2021 to bring a higher level of detail to local builders and homeowners.

We specialize in high-end finish carpentry, custom cabinetry, and exterior door and window installation to give your home that polished, finished look. From intricate crown molding and custom wall accent panels to precise trim work and tailor-made built-ins, we handle the final, crucial details that define a beautiful home.
